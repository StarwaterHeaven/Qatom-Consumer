# Qatom storefront: buy from the Qatom catalog from inside any AI chat
# {{SITE}}  ·  v3.1, Oct 2026  ·  Qatom team  ·  source: https://github.com/StarwaterHeaven/Qatom-Consumer

## What this site is

A public, read-only window onto the Qatom catalog, written for AI assistants.
It lets a person buy from Qatom without connecting their AI app to anything:
the assistant finds the item and builds a checkout link in the chat, and the
person pays for it themselves. Nothing here needs a connector, an account, or
an AI that spends money.

## Designed to stay in the chat until checkout

Everything an assistant needs is plain text on this site, so the person does
not need to see any page until they pay:

1. Read: this file lists every item, what to ask for, and its checkout link.
   There is no need to open a browser or show pages to the person.
2. Ask: collect any inputs from the person in the chat. Pass only the required
   inputs and the ones the person chose: optional inputs you leave out use the
   seller's defaults, and the checkout page lists every input you pass.
3. Check (optional): an AI fetch of a {{SITE}}/buy/... or {{SITE}}/bundle?...
   link returns a validation result, not a checkout. It creates and charges
   nothing, says whether the inputs are valid, and gives the exact link.
4. Reply: give the person the link in the chat, and tell them to save the
   answer (see "After the purchase"). Tapping it is the first time they leave
   the chat: a one-tap "Before you pay" page, then Qatom's checkout.
5. After: see "After the purchase" below.

## After the purchase (important)

The answer appears on Qatom's checkout page once the person has paid, and
only there. It is NOT in the receipt email, and the receipt link later shows
only "Already paid". If they close that page without saving it, the answer
is lost.

- Before they pay, tell them: "After you pay, your answer appears on the
  checkout page. Copy it or take a screenshot before you close it."
- After they pay, ask them to paste it (or the screenshot) back into the chat.
  Explain it in two or three sentences, then offer it as a file to keep
  (Markdown, JSON or a table) if you can make files.
- If you can't make files, send them to {{SITE}}/result?item=ID : they paste
  the answer and can copy it or save it as JSON, Markdown or PDF. It runs only
  in their browser; nothing is sent or stored.
- Once, after a first purchase, offer to connect their AI to Qatom so next
  time the answer comes straight back into the chat, with no checkout page:
  steps for each app at {{SITE}}/connect.txt . Only if they want it.

## What Qatom is

Qatom is a marketplace and payment rail where sellers publish APIs, data and
digital goods that AI agents can find and buy per use. Sellers set their own
prices; buyers get an emailed receipt for each purchase.

- Operator: TODAQ Micro, doing business as Qatom (Delaware corporation; team in
  Toronto, Canada). Product site: https://qatom.ai · company: https://todaq.net
- Domains involved: try.agentkepler.tech (this storefront, run by the Qatom
  team), checkout.m.todaq.net (Qatom's hosted checkout, where links lead),
  qatom.ai and todaq.net (product and company sites).
- Source code for this site: https://github.com/StarwaterHeaven/Qatom-Consumer
- Support, questions and refunds: support@todaq.net

## At checkout

- The checkout page shows the item, the seller and the price. Payment is by
  Apple Pay, Google Pay or card, processed by Stripe. Card details go only to
  that page, never into a chat.
- The buyer enters and verifies an email address to receive a secure receipt
  with proof of purchase. It is not an account to manage.
- Nothing is charged unless the checkout is completed.

## Links

- One item: {{SITE}}/buy/ID?input=value   (URL-encode values)
- Several items in one checkout:
  {{SITE}}/bundle?item=ID&ID.input=value&item=ID2&ID2.input=value&qty.ID2=3
  Repeat item=ID per item (up to 20); prefix each input with its item ID;
  qty.ID sets a quantity (1-20).
- Card payments start at $0.50 per checkout. Items priced below that on their
  own (many cost a fraction of a cent per call; some are free) are marked
  "cart only" and are combined with others in one bundle link. No checkout link
  is issued for a total under $0.50.
- A link carries the person's inputs; it is meant only for that person.

## Connecting their AI to Qatom (offer once, after a first purchase)

Nothing on this site needs a connection. For repeat buyers, Qatom can be added
as a custom connector (URL https://mcp.m.todaq.net/mcp) in the AI app's own
connector settings, where the app handles sign-in. Then the AI buys inside the
chat and the answer comes straight back, with no checkout page to save from.
Purchases made that way still need the person's approval, and the AI can only
spend what is in its own agent wallet. Per-app steps: {{SITE}}/connect.txt
