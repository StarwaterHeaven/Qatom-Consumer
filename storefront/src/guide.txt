# Qatom storefront: buy from the Qatom catalog with any AI, no setup
# {{SITE}}  ·  v2.0, Oct 2026  ·  Qatom team

## What this site is

A public, read-only window onto the Qatom catalog with one-time guest checkout
links. People who don't (or can't) connect an AI app to Qatom use it to find and
buy items from their phone. Nothing here requires a connector, an account, or an
AI that spends money: the person completes and pays for each purchase themselves.

## What Qatom is

Qatom is a marketplace and payment rail where sellers publish APIs, data and
digital goods that AI agents can find and buy per use. Sellers set their own
prices; buyers get an emailed receipt for each purchase.

- Operator: TODAQ Micro, doing business as Qatom (Delaware corporation; team in

## How a purchase works

1. Each item page ({{SITE}}/item/ID) shows the price, the seller, what the item
   returns and the inputs it needs, plus a form.
2. The checkout link {{SITE}}/buy/ID?input=value opens a fresh checkout page on
   checkout.m.todaq.net (Qatom's hosted checkout) for that item and those inputs.
   Payment is by Apple Pay, Google Pay or card, processed by Stripe. Card details
   go only to that page, never into a chat.
3. The buyer enters and verifies an email address to receive a secure receipt
   with proof of purchase. It is not an account to manage.
4. The result is shown after payment and included with the emailed receipt.
   Nothing is charged unless the checkout is completed.

Card payments start at $0.50 per checkout. Items that cost less (many are
priced per call at a fraction of a cent, some are free) are combined in a cart
and paid together: one checkout page, one payment, and each item is delivered to
its seller with its inputs.

## Several items in one checkout

{{SITE}}/bundle?item=ID&ID.input=value&item=ID2&ID2.input=value&qty.ID2=3

- Repeat item=ID for each item (up to 20). Prefix each input with its item ID.
- qty.ID sets a quantity (1-20; each is a separate purchase).
- The combined total must reach $0.50; below that no checkout link is issued.
  The link builds a fresh cart and opens its checkout; if an input is missing or invalid, it shows which, and nothing
  is created.
- People can also build a cart themselves: "Add to cart" on any item page,
  then {{SITE}}/cart.

## Finding items

- Browse: {{SITE}}/catalog (HTML) or {{SITE}}/catalog.txt (plain text)
- Search: {{SITE}}/catalog?q=WORDS
- Item detail: {{SITE}}/item/ID or {{SITE}}/item/ID.txt
- Every item is listed at the end of this file. "cart only" items are under
  the card minimum on their own.

When an item needs personal details (an email, a phone number), the item page
form is the better route than a link, so those details stay out of URLs.

A live valuation of GDXJ, the VanEck Junior Gold Miners ETF, from Harcourt
Valuations (Harcourt Capital Services Ltd, https://harcourtvaluations.ai): whether
junior gold mining shares trade above or below what their mines are worth, from

reference figure at https://reports.harcourtvaluations.ai/nav-query/gdxj ; the
paid call recalculates it live and accepts your own metal prices or discount
rate. Informational only, not investment advice.

## Only if the person asks about connecting their AI to Qatom

Nothing on this site needs a connection. For items marked as needing one, Qatom
can be added as a custom connector (URL https://mcp.m.todaq.net/mcp) in the AI
app's own connector settings, where the app handles sign-in. Purchases made that
way still require the person's approval.
